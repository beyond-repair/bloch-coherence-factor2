## Distinction that stays frozen

\[
\boxed{
\text{factor of 2 is an observed structural ratio in this model}
\neq
\text{factor of 2 is a universal constant of nature}.
}
\]

The left-hand statement is Theorems A–C.
The right-hand statement is not a theorem of this repository.

\[

\frac{16|\varepsilon_*|}{\lambda}.
\]

The scalar quartic channel can restore its own Bloch gap once
fluctuations are included. The realization then has to face the

in this repository.

**Branch U (systematic under-restoration).**

\[
\varphi_{\rm loop}^2
<
\frac{16|\varepsilon_*|}{\lambda}
\quad\text{everywhere in the condensing grid.}
\]

Then the scalar quartic channel systematically under-restores
its own spectral instability. That is more valuable than another
failed numerical experiment. It motivates testing whether the
missing factor is supplied by a *collective-sector* interaction,
rather than arbitrarily adding another scalar coupling.
