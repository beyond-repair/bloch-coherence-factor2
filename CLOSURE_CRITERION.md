# Closure criterion for later P3 realizations

Do not broaden the collective sector indefinitely.

## Admissibility

A new P3 realization (C4, C5, …) is admissible only if its
**field content, symmetry, coupling structure, and action are
specified independently of the frozen target Hessian**.

Independent of the frozen target Hessian means:

- the Lagrangian does not contain \(V_{\rm fluc}^{\rm fr}\) as
  a copied function,
- no multiplier or kernel is fixed by demanding
  \(\Omega_-^2=\varepsilon_*/2\),
- the field content is justified by a symmetry, constraint,
  conservation law, or other principle that can be stated
  without mentioning Theorems A–C.

If those fail, the candidate is a COUNTERTERM. COUNTERTERM is
not a parent. It is P2 in variational language.

E_{\rm extra}

0,

Then apply the five-question gate of

## What a pass would mean

If some future Cn is INDEPENDENT-PASS and then survives the
five questions, it is a new physical hypothesis. The quantum
prescription may be built from *that* action.

It is not a mathematical patch whose only job is to manufacture
\(-V_{\rm fluc}^{\rm fr}\).

## What a continuing fail means

The ledger statement stands:

\[
\varphi_{\rm loop}^2
\text{ is undefined within the tested theory space.}
\]

The space has grown by one named action. The loop is still
blocked.
